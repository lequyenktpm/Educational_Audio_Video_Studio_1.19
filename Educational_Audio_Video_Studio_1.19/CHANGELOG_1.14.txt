Educational Audio Video Studio 1.14 – 2026-11-22

NEW: Reading Along Studio
- Added a dedicated Reading Along Studio tab.
- Offline topic templates for English and Japanese sample reading passages.
- Editable lesson title and text; import TXT, MD, PDF and EPUB.
- Automatic open-book page layout and generated pointing-hand graphic; no hand image/video asset required.
- Word highlight + pointer tracking for English; short phrase-chunk tracking for Japanese.
- Select TTS audio as the soundtrack and export vertical 720x1280 / 24 fps MP4.
- Estimated timing works offline. Optional faster-whisper alignment for closer English word timing.
- Added install_alignment.bat and pypdf dependency for PDF text extraction.

PRESERVED from 1.13
- Priority voiceover with audio ducking, built-in ambient SFX, music mix, karaoke subtitles, custom outro, text watermark and whole-video visual effects.

LIMITATIONS
- Offline lesson generation uses built-in templates, not an LLM.
- Estimated timing is not forced alignment; Whisper option is English-focused and downloads its model on first use.
- Japanese uses phrase chunks rather than dictionary-grade morphological word segmentation.
- Reading Along output is vertical 720x1280 only; no real filmed person/avatar or timeline editor.
- Windows GUI has not been tested on a physical Windows machine.
