Educational Audio Video Studio 1.18
==================================

Reading Along sync
- Replaced duration-only frame timing with an absolute word-onset timeline.
- Keeps the active word/pointer visible through inter-word pauses and breaths.
- Adds an unhighlighted opening state for leading silence and holds the last word to audio end.
- Uses character-based matching between transcript text and Whisper word/phrase timestamps.
- Supports English word timestamps and Japanese phrase/character timing when faster-whisper returns segment timestamps.
- Enables Whisper alignment by default and clarifies the estimated-timing fallback.
- Prevents small concat/fps rounding differences from truncating the narration tail.

Layout
- Scrollable tab content now stretches to at least the viewport height.
- Short tabs no longer leave a large empty region below their controls.
- Reading Along input and preview panes fill the available tab height.
- Scrollbars remain conditional on actual overflow.

Book page
- Increased reading text size and line spacing.
- Kept the text area and pointer clear of the footer and progress rail.
- Preserved the existing palettes and illustration styles.
