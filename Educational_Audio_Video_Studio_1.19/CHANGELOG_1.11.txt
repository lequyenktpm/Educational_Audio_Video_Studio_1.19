Educational Audio Video Studio 1.11
==================================

New in 1.11
- Expanded Add Karaoke Sub into a video finishing workflow.
- Added 5 whole-video visual treatments: cinematic fade, slow zoom, soft vignette, warm film, cool tech.
- Added optional outro text overlay for the final 0-6 seconds with fade-to-dark.
- Added original audio mute and original volume control.
- Added experimental stereo center-cancel dialogue reduction with explicit limitation checks.
- Added optional looping background music and ambient SFX tracks with independent volume controls.
- Added dedicated karaoke render progress bar and status text.
- Made the Add Karaoke Sub settings panel vertically scrollable for smaller displays.
- Preserved source video; output remains a separate MP4.

Validation
- Python compile passed.
- FFmpeg smoke tests passed for all 5 video effect presets.
- Audio mix smoke test passed with original audio + background music + ambient SFX.
- Mute-original + music/SFX output passed.
- No full Windows GUI runtime test was performed.

Limitations
- Video presets apply to the whole clip and its beginning/end; they do not create inter-shot transitions without edit points.
- Center-cancel dialogue reduction is experimental and may remove centered instruments; it is not AI vocal separation.
- Word highlighting remains estimated when subtitles contain only sentence-level timestamps.
